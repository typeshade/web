02 / UNDER THE HOOD

How TypeAudio processes sound

TypeAudio owns the audio semantics. TypeShade compiles the kernels and runs them on the selected backend. The playground uses the TypeAudio engine, with its source version pinned in the web repository.

Read the design →
  1. 01

    Compose the voice

    Mix two oscillators, detune a bank of voices, then shape the tone with a resonant lowpass and ADSR.

  2. 02

    Render in blocks

    Run the voice bank on CPU or WebGPU. Add drive, delay, and a room reverb to the rendered signal.

  3. 03

    Listen and inspect

    Compare before and after effects, inspect the spectrum, and save a patch for your next experiment.
